The Rules of Blackjack.

How it Works.

In Blackjack, the goal is to get the value of you hand close to or equal to 21. The numbered cards are worth their face value of 2-10. The face cards (ie Kings, Queens, and Jacks) are worth 10. Finally, the Ace is worth 1 or 11 as per player's choice. The Dealer and each player is dealt two cards. The Dealer has one card face up and one face down. Each player faces their card up and must decide to "Stand", "Hit", or "Split", until they are close to 21, without exceeding. The player (Dealer) with the hand closest to 21 wins.

What is Busting?

If the value of your hand (or cards) exceeds 21, then you bust and lose.
